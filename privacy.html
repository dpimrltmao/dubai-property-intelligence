Privacy, clearly explained

DPI is an independent research publication. You can read the site without accepting optional analytics.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 and Metricool to understand which pages and tools readers use. These services may process device, browser, page and interaction information and set identifiers if you choose to accept. Neither tracker loads before you accept optional analytics on this website.

Your choice

You can accept or reject analytics and change your decision using the “Privacy choices” control at the bottom of any page. Rejecting does not prevent you from reading DPI.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the DPI newsletter, your email address is used to deliver requested updates through our email-service provider. Newsletter subscription is separate from your analytics choice.

Providers and retention

Google and Metricool process analytics information under their own policies. Data may be processed outside the UAE. Retention depends on provider and account settings; request details or ask for assistance using the contact below. Google privacy policy; Metricool privacy policy.
