# Purpose

The purpose of this framework is to ensure that every Dubai Property Intelligence report is consistent, evidence-based, transparent, and useful for property advisors, investors, and decision-makers.

The framework exists to transform information into actionable intelligence.

---

# Editorial Philosophy

News tells people what happened.

Intelligence explains:

• Why it happened.
• Why it matters.
• What happens next.
• What clients should do.

Every report produced by DPI must answer these questions.

---

# The Five-Step Intelligence Model

## Step 1 – Gather

Collect information only from trusted sources.

Examples include:

- Dubai Land Department
- Dubai Statistics Center
- UAE Central Bank
- IMF
- World Bank
- Reuters
- Bloomberg
- CBRE
- Knight Frank
- JLL

---

## Step 2 – Verify

Never rely on a single source when independent confirmation is possible.

If information cannot be verified, label it clearly.

Confidence Levels:

Verified
Confirmed
Estimated
Early Signal
Opinion

---

## Step 3 – Analyse

Every important story must answer:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

Why does it matter?

Who is affected?

What should property advisors tell clients today?

---

## Step 4 – Score

Every story receives a DPI Relevance Score.

Source Credibility (0–5)

Dubai Market Impact (0–5)

Urgency (0–5)

Confidence (0–5)

Advisor Value (0–5)

Maximum Score: 25

21–25 = Front Page

16–20 = Main Report

11–15 = Brief Mention

0–10 = Ignore

---

## Step 5 – Publish

Every published report must satisfy:

Accurate

Evidence-based

Easy to understand

Actionable

Professionally designed

---

# DPI Golden Rules

1. Never invent data.

2. Separate facts from opinions.

3. Every statistic must have a traceable source.

4. Forecasts must be labelled as forecasts.

5. Corrections are published openly.

6. Simplicity is a competitive advantage.

7. Trust is more valuable than speed.

---

# The Three Layer Rule

Every major story should contain:

Layer 1

Facts

Layer 2

Analysis

Layer 3

Client Action
