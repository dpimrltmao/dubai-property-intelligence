Wealth Perseverance Index

One score. Three parts.

WPI simplifies several historical market signals into one reading without pretending to predict what happens next.

50%

Growth

Measures historical price growth across multiple time horizons.

35%

Stability

Measures historical drawdowns and consistency of positive performance.

15%

Calmer Movement

Gives additional weight to communities with less volatile historical price movement.

WPI is not an expected return, probability of profit, or recommendation to buy a particular community.
