## Important progress since 3 November kickoff
The October 2026 **dated monthly edition is now available**, published 9 November 2026:
- Dubai Real Estate Data, October 2026 edition: https://www.dubairealestatedata.com/report/2026-10
- The edition states DLD residential registered October sales **11,735** / **AED 31.8bn** as known at the **9 November 2026 registration cutoff**.
- The **AED 1,738/sqft** median and **+5.5% YoY** refer to **rolling 12 months to 31 October**, **not October-only median/yoy**.
- The **67.7% off-plan share** likewise describes 12-month registered sales, not October 2026 alone.
- Its stated methodology uses outlier-trimmed medians and accounts for registration lag. Source publication and cutoffs are published, but the exact query and underlying data have not been independently audited by DPI.

## Conflicting October headline counts: do not reconcile by cherry picking
- Dubai Real Estate Data: **11,735**, residential sales, data through 9 Nov; https://www.dubairealestatedata.com/report/2026-10
- Property Monitor numbers as cited by AKT (article dated 5 Nov): **11,402**, residential sale registrations, **AED 28.34bn**; https://www.aktrealestate.ae/en/news/dubai-property-prices-october-2026
- Seafront DLD-based broader property sales: **11,976**, **AED 35.0bn**; https://www.seafront.ae/market/dubai-property-market-report-october-2026
These are **not automatically comparable**: different reporting dates, populations (residential versus wider property types), filters, off-plan classification, and registration revisions may account for differences. No reconciliation proof has yet been completed.
