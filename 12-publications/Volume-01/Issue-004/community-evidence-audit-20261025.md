## Decision in brief

**ACCEPT:** Three *community screening* examples using one frozen, like-defined ready resale data product.  
**ACCEPT WITH CAVEAT:** Al Furjan two-definition contrast from a separate dated 19 October product snapshot.  
**REJECT:** Framing any area as a “proven bargain” or property-level discount.  
**DEFER:** Matched-building/bedroom/floor deal comparisons until raw DLD sales and property attributes can be joined and reviewed.

## Evidence matrix — one frozen snapshot

**Source:** Dubai Real Estate Data, September 2026 edition (published 15 Oct; data as registered by 12 Oct 2026). Source data described as DLD via Dubai Pulse. URL: https://www.dubairealestatedata.com/report/2026-09

**Statistic:** Median AED per square foot among ready-resale apartments and villas **per area** in the trailing 12 months ending 30 Sept 2026; annual change versus preceding 12 months. Minimum **50 ready sales in each comparison window**; area-specific exact sample sizes for the two windows are not exposed in that frozen summary.

| Area | Ready resale median AED/ft² | T12M vs prior T12M | Source confidence | Investment inference |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Dubai Science Park | 1,375 | −9.7% | Provider-reported, same sample rule | Area-level price-momentum *screen* only |
| Al Furjan | 1,144 | −5.4% | Provider-reported, same sample rule | Area-level price-momentum *screen* only |
| Al Kifaf | 1,947 | −5.3% | Provider-reported, same sample rule | Area-level price-momentum *screen* only |

**Not a paired / matched-unit index:** Resale-only filtering removes the biggest off-plan-launch mix effect, but changes in which *ready* homes sell — tower, floor, size, view and quality — can still move the median.

**Important distinction:** The 15 October **frozen September edition** is selected for editorial reproducibility. The provider's updated weekly 19 October data represents different rolling windows and should not be substituted into the frozen comparative table.

## The key editorial chart: 'One area, opposite headlines'

**Source:** Dubai Real Estate Data *Areas* and *Market overview*, updated 19 Oct 2026, based on data through 16 Oct.  
https://www.dubairealestatedata.com/areas  
https://www.dubairealestatedata.com/market

For **Al Furjan**:
- **All registered built-residential sales:** median price per square foot **+14.2% YoY**.
- **Ready resale apartments/villas only:** median price per square foot **−7.0% YoY**; current ready-sale sample shown: **1,756 sales** (the prior-window sample count is not exposed in the overview).
- **Interpretation:** Different transaction populations, with off-plan launches influencing all-sales mix. This is not evidence that a particular unit lost 7% of its value or that sellers discount by 7%.

Use these two figures **side by side only if each group is labeled**; no inference that the difference (21.2 percentage points) is a causal off-plan premium. Shifting stock mix within categories also matters.
