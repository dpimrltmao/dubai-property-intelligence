A simple buyer checklist

Before calling it a good investment, ask four questions.

Keep these questions close when looking at a listing, launch, or attractive return forecast.

01

What actually sold?

Check recent completed sales for genuinely similar homes.

02

What income remains?

Consider vacancies, service charges, running costs, and finance—not just headline rent.

03

What has really been delivered?

Look at building progress and real handovers, not only promised dates.

04

Who will buy it later?

Think ahead about resale demand, competition, and your exit.
