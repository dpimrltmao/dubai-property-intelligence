## Non-negotiable definitions
- Data is DLD raw publisher-provided extracts: transaction and rental 20 Nov 2026; projects 1 Nov 2026. Jan-Oct 2025/2026 identical windows.
- Rental counts = starts of qualifying one-property contracts, **not** unique new households, registered demand, occupancy or net migration.
- Existing/Sell registration subset **not certified resale-only**. Registered sales price medians are not mix-adjusted. Volumes may differ due to registration lags.
- Active project 2027 registered end dates **not** verified completed residential handovers; project counts do not represent dwellings, and registered progress cannot forecast delays.
- DLD area IDs may cover multiple branded districts. Validate reader mapping; no area is recommended solely from these data.
- No absorption, vacancy or citywide household-formation rate can be inferred by merging sales, rents and projects.
- Prefer a concise November issue with evidenced comparisons rather than rushing full 2027 supply census. Save the deeper validated building/unit and delivery analysis for future quarterly issues.
