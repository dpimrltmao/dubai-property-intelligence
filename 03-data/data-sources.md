**Publication:** Dubai Property Intelligence

---

# 1. Purpose

The DPI Data Source Register defines the preferred sources used to research, verify and analyze information for Dubai Property Intelligence.

The objective is:

> Use the most authoritative and reliable source available for each claim.

Sources must be evaluated for:

- Authority
- Accuracy
- Recency
- Relevance
- Transparency
- Primary-source status

---

# 2. Source Priority

DPI uses the following general hierarchy.

## Tier 1 — Primary / Official Sources

Highest priority.

Examples:

- Government authorities
- Regulators
- Central banks
- Official statistical agencies
- Official international organizations
- Official company announcements

Whenever possible, DPI should use the primary source rather than a secondary article reporting on it.

# 12. Rumors

Unverified claims may be monitored.

They must NOT be presented as confirmed information.

Potential rumors may be discussed under:

# Rumor vs Reality

The report must clearly distinguish:

- Rumor
- Confirmed information
- Unknown / unverified information

---

# 13. Source Verification

Before using an important statistic, DPI should attempt to determine:

1. Who produced the information?
2. What is the original source?
3. When was it published?
4. What period does the data cover?
5. Is it current?
6. Is the methodology clear?
7. Can it be independently verified?
8. Are reputable sources reporting the same information?

---

# 14. Date Rules

Every important data point should be associated with a date.

DPI must distinguish between:

- Publication date
- Data period
- Announcement date
- Forecast period

For example:

A report published in August 2026 may contain June 2026 data.

DPI must not describe June data as "August data."

---

# 15. Conflicting Sources

If reputable sources disagree:

1. Prefer the primary source where available.
2. Check the methodology.
3. Check the date.
4. Determine whether the sources are measuring different things.
5. Explain the disagreement when material.

DPI must not hide meaningful disagreement.

---

# 16. Source Citations

Important statistics and factual claims should have a source.

Where practical, the final report should identify:

- Source
- Publication / report title
- Date
- Relevant data period

Digital versions may include clickable source links.

---

# 17. Source Quality Score

DPI may eventually assign sources a quality score.

Example:

### A — Primary / Official

Government, regulator, central bank or official statistical source.

### B — Highly Reputable Secondary

Major financial publication or established research institution.

### C — Specialist Secondary

Recognized real estate or industry publication.

### D — Unverified / Informal

Social media, forums, anonymous claims or unsupported commentary.

Important DPI statistics should preferably use A or B sources.

---

# 18. AI Research Rule

AI-generated information is NOT itself a source.

AI may:

- Search
- Compare
- Summarize
- Analyze
- Calculate
- Explain

But the underlying factual claim should come from a real source.

The AI must never create a citation for a source it did not actually verify.

---

# 19. No Fabricated Sources

The following are prohibited:

- Invented statistics
- Invented reports
- Invented quotes
- Invented URLs
- Invented transaction data
- Invented analyst opinions
- Fake citations

If information cannot be verified:

> Say that it cannot be verified.

# 21. Future Automation

The eventual DPI automation should:

1. Collect new information.
2. Identify relevant stories.
3. Record source and publication date.
4. Compare information across sources.
5. Verify important claims.
6. Extract relevant data.
7. Analyze potential Dubai-property impact.
8. Assign confidence.
9. Feed verified information into the report-generation system.

The automation must not publish automatically if important information is uncertain or contradictory without appropriate review rules.

---

# 22. Editorial Independence

A source should never receive preferential treatment because it is commercially useful to DPI.

DPI must prioritize evidence over advertising, relationships or promotional claims.
