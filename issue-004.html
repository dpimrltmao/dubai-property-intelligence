Dubai's Buyer Opportunity: Real or Illusion?

Sales are slowing. But are buyers actually getting better deals?
Read the magazine PDFMarket evidence
Quick insight

A slower market does not mean every home is a bargain. Buyer opportunity depends on actual comparable sales, achievable income, costs and the exit strategy.

01

The market is slowing — not uniformly

Savills recorded approximately 34,500 residential transactions in Q3 2026, down 4% quarter-on-quarter, with AED 70.8 billion in transaction value. ValuStrat's September index showed residential values down 4.5% year-on-year and 0.1% month-on-month. These different measures cannot be treated as identical.

02

One area, two different stories

In a historical 19 October Al Furjan data snapshot, the median for all registered built-home sales increased 14.2% year-on-year, while the ready-resale-only median declined 7.0%. These are different populations. The frozen September report independently recorded a -5.4% ready-resale comparison. Neither figure values an individual unit.

03

Three communities to investigate, not to recommend

The frozen September ready-resale data screen reported Dubai Science Park at AED 1,375/sq ft (-9.7% annually), Al Furjan at AED 1,144/sq ft (-5.4%) and Al Kifaf at AED 1,947/sq ft (-5.3%). These medians compare trailing twelve-month periods and require at least 50 ready sales in each period. Building-level evidence is necessary before claiming a genuine bargain.

04

Why a 5% discount may be an illusion

An illustrative AED 1 million property collecting AED 80,000 a year in rent with AED 15,000 annual operating costs has a 6.50% net operating yield. A 5% lower purchase price raises that to 6.84%, if income holds. If collected rent instead falls to AED 72,000, the yield becomes 6.00%. This excludes financing, acquisition costs and applicable taxes.

05

Five checks before making an offer

Ask what comparable properties actually sold for; what achievable net rent is; the full purchase cost; which legal, supply, project and maintenance risks exist; and how liquid the resale market might be. Planned supply is not completed supply: Savills reports over 525,000 homes scheduled for 2027–2030, not guaranteed deliveries.

06

Our conclusion

Buy the evidence, not the headline. This independent educational analysis is not personalized financial, legal or tax advice. DPI has not independently reconstructed the provider indices or verified a specific property as a bargain.

Historical-snapshot note

The 19 October Al Furjan comparison is a dated historical extract. The linked market board updates, so its current figures may differ. The frozen September report remains fixed as published.
