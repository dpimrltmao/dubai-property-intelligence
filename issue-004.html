03 / Market reality

Fewer sales.
A more selective market.

A transaction count tells us how active the market is. An index attempts to estimate how values have moved. Neither determines a specific apartment's fair purchase price.

04 / The headline illusion

One area.
Two very different stories.

Al Furjan demonstrates why the type of transaction matters more than the headline alone.

In a historical data snapshot dated 19 October 2026, the same provider reported a positive median change for all registered built-home sales and a negative median change for ready resale only.

These are different transaction populations. Neither is a matched-unit price index or proof of a discount on an individual home.

07 / The buyer opportunity test

Five questions before you buy.

A promising asking-price reduction is not the same as a defensible purchase.

01

What actually sold?

Recent registered sales of closely comparable properties.

02

What will it earn?

Achievable rent, vacancy and operating expenses.

03

What's the full cost?

Purchase price, fees, finance and fit-out.

04

What could go wrong?

Building, supply, legal and delivery risks.

05

Who buys it later?

Resale demand, liquidity and realistic exit options.
