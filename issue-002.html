Dubai Oversupply
Real or Hype?

Dubai is building fast. But does more supply automatically mean falling prices?

Issue 002
Published 28 September 2026
Read / Download Issue 002 Back to DPI

DPI Issue 002

Look beyond the oversupply headline.

SUPPLY EVIDENCE

24,537 units completed

Supply is rising — but what does that actually mean?

Dubai completed 24,537 residential units during H1 2026, approximately 36% more than the same period a year earlier. More supply matters, but the headline alone does not tell us where market pressure will appear.

COMMUNITY ANALYSIS

Pressure is not equal everywhere

Where is pressure actually building?

Dubai is not one property market. New supply affects communities differently depending on existing stock, future handovers, buyer demand, rental demand and the type of property being delivered.

RUMOR VS REALITY

More homes ≠ automatic crash

Separate the narrative from the evidence.

Rising supply can increase competition and create pressure in particular segments, but oversupply should be tested community by community rather than assumed from a citywide headline.

Investor Lens

Three questions before you shortlist.

1. How much competing supply is entering this specific community?

2. Is real end-user and rental demand absorbing that supply?

3. Does the individual property still make sense at its current price?

A Dubai-wide supply number is the beginning of the analysis — not the investment decision.
